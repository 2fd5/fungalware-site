# Fungalware in-world concepts

Owner brainstorm, 23 October 2026. These are parked concepts, not committed
Moving Oasis features or claims about real hardware.

- Reuse the selected fungal/circuit studio emblem on fictional cartridges,
  magazines, leaflets or a small in-world Fungalware advertisement.
- A living fungal-core synthesizer: its oscillator is a living fungal cell or
  mycelial culture. The owner feeds it; neglect can kill the organism and silence
  the instrument. Explore the relationship between nourishment and sound before
  deciding whether it belongs in the game.
- Loose thematic phrase: a fusion of mycelium and brain / worlds. Naming and
  final advertising copy remain undecided.
