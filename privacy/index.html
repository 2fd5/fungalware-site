The newsletter

If you sign up for Moving Oasis news, we keep your email address and the record of your consent (when you signed up and confirmed, and the IP address used). We use it only to send news about our games, a few times a year. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

Signing up takes two steps: you submit the form, then confirm with the link we email you. Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect emails sent before.

We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe we may keep the address only as "unsubscribed", so we never email you again by mistake, unless you ask us to erase it completely.

Who processes it for us

The list is stored and the emails are sent by MailerLite (UAB "MailerLite", Lithuania, EU) under a data processing agreement. The signup form loads a script from MailerLite, which receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads and when you submit. MailerLite may use sub-processors outside the EU under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and served through Cloudflare, Inc. Like any web host, they process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and our fonts are served from this site.
