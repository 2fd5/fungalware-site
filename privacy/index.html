The newsletter

If you sign up for Moving Oasis news, we keep your email address and the record of your consent (when you signed up and confirmed, and the IP address used). We use it only to send news about our games, a few times a year. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

Signing up takes two steps: you submit the form, then confirm with the link we email you. Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect emails sent before.

We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe we may keep the address only as "unsubscribed", so we never email you again by mistake, unless you ask us to erase it completely.

MailerLite may use sub-processors outside the EU under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Moving Oasis playtest: feedback and session data

In the playtest and demo builds you can send us two things from inside the game. Nothing is sent unless you choose it.

  • Feedback (Pause → Feedback): the words you write, with the build version, your system (Steam Deck, Linux or Windows), the map and the minutes you played.
  • Session data: while you play, the game keeps a log on your own computer of your button presses and what happened in the game (cities, creatures, upgrades). It is sent only if you pick "Session data: included" in Feedback or "Send & exit" when you quit. No video, screenshots, name, Steam account or device ID. The game keeps your last five sessions and deletes older ones; a log you never send never leaves your computer. What is inside, with examples.

We use both only to find what is confusing or broken and to replay runs while we tune the game. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given by choosing to send. They are stored by Supabase on servers in Ireland (EU) under a data processing agreement; like any server, it sees the IP address of the upload. We delete session data after the game's release. To have a session deleted, write to hello@fungalware.com or on the Steam forum with the folder name the game shows (a date and time).

Playtest Moving Oasis: opinie i dane sesji

W wersjach playtest i demo możesz wysłać nam z gry dwie rzeczy. Bez Twojego wyboru nic nie jest wysyłane.

  • Opinia (Pauza → Feedback): napisany przez Ciebie tekst, wersja gry, system (Steam Deck, Linux lub Windows), mapa i liczba minut gry.
  • Dane sesji: podczas gry gra zapisuje na Twoim komputerze, jakie przyciski naciskasz i co dzieje się w grze (miasta, stworzenia, ulepszenia). Wysyła je tylko, jeśli wybierzesz „Session data: included” w Feedback albo „Send & exit” przy wyjściu. Bez wideo, zrzutów ekranu, imienia, konta Steam i identyfikatora urządzenia. Gra trzyma pięć ostatnich sesji i usuwa starsze; niewysłany zapis nigdy nie opuszcza Twojego komputera.

Używamy ich wyłącznie, żeby znaleźć to, co niejasne lub zepsute, i odtwarzać rozgrywki przy strojeniu gry. Podstawą prawną jest Twoja zgoda (art. 6 ust. 1 lit. a RODO), wyrażona wyborem wysyłki. Przechowuje je Supabase na serwerach w Irlandii (UE) na podstawie umowy powierzenia; jak każdy serwer widzi adres IP wysyłki. Dane sesji usuwamy po premierze gry. Aby usunąć sesję, napisz na hello@fungalware.com albo na forum Steam, podając nazwę folderu, którą pokazuje gra (data i godzina).
