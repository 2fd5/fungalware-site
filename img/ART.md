# Fungalware studio emblem

`fungalware-logo.png` is the owner-selected second logo iteration, 23 October
2026. It combines a mushroom and mycelium with printed-circuit traces and
terminal pads, using the website's teal, cream and purple palette.

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool under the owner's direction.
The transparent master and earlier alternative are preserved externally in
`/home/magic/dev/moving-oasis-media/brand/fungalware/`. This checked-in copy
is required by the static website and has no dependency on that local archive.

Keep the emblem small beside the studio name in the footer. Future cartridge,
magazine and merchandise uses are possibilities, not current game changes.
